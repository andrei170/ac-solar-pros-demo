Why we stopped selling wind, and why we are back

We installed bladed wind turbines years ago. In our climate they failed too often, and the companies that made them went under. So we stopped selling them.

The new vertical designs are different: built to keep working through our winters and our wind, with no long blades to fail. We waited years for one we were willing to put our name on. ⚠ Turbine model, rated output and manufacturer warranty

Why wind with solar

Solar is strongest in summer and during the day. Wind often picks up at night and through the winter. Together, with a battery, the system covers far more of the year.
