// Three ways to make power

Sun, wind and storage. One company.

Most solar companies stop at the panels. We size the whole system: what the sun gives you, what the wind adds in the dark months, and the batteries that hold it for when you need it.

Solar

The sun does the heavy lifting

Eastern Washington is not the rainy side of the state. Rooftop, ground mount and carport systems, designed around your bill and your roof or land. ⚠ Your sun-hours figure, with its source

Wind · new for 2027

Wind fills the gap

Vertical wind turbines that keep turning through winter nights and storms, when panels are quiet. Built to survive our climate, which the old bladed turbines did not.

Storage

Batteries keep the lights on

Store what you make and use it at night, in an outage, or all the time if you are off-grid. Fewer generator runs, fewer propane deliveries.

// Off-grid, for real

Bought land with no power? Start here.

Bringing the grid to a ridge, a canyon or forty acres in Klickitat County can cost more than the power system itself. We design solar, storage and wind together so a homestead can run on its own, without a generator running all night or a propane truck every month.

  • Sized from what you will actually run: well pump, shop, house
  • Batteries for the nights and the cloudy weeks
  • Wind in the mix for winter, from 2027
  • One quote you can take to the bank for the build loan
